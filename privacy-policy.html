Privacy Policy

YouTube Gemini Summarizer

Last updated: February 17, 2026

No data collected

Overview

YouTube Gemini Summarizer is a browser extension that adds a one-click "Summarize" button to YouTube videos. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing information.

What the Extension Does

  • Reads the current YouTube video URL when you click the "Summarize" button.
  • Passes that URL to Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) within your own browser.

What the Extension Does NOT Do

  • Collect any personal information.
  • Track your browsing history or activity.
  • Send data to any external server or third party.
  • Use cookies, analytics, or telemetry of any kind.
  • Store any data beyond the current browser session.

Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions, used solely for core functionality:

  • tabs — to find, create, and reuse a Gemini tab instead of opening duplicates.
  • Host access to youtube.com — to inject the "Summarize" button on video pages.
  • Host access to gemini.google.com — to fill in and submit the summarization prompt automatically.

Third-Party Services

The extension opens Google Gemini in your browser to generate summaries. Your use of Google Gemini is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. This extension itself does not send any data to Google or any other party — it simply automates what you would do manually (copy a URL and paste it into Gemini).

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
